# Meta Ads Copy Document - Portfolio Studio (7 Products)

**Budget:** ~$35/product ($5/day x 7 days per product)
**Goal:** >6% email capture rate on landing pages
**Primary Market:** English-speaking professionals (US, UK, CA, AU, remote workers globally)

---

## General Meta Ads Setup Tips for $35 Budget

### Campaign Structure
- **Objective:** Use "Leads" or "Traffic" objective (NOT "Conversions" -- you need volume at this budget to learn)
- **Ad Set:** 1 ad set per product, broad targeting with interest stacking (do NOT split into multiple ad sets at $5/day)
- **Ads:** Run all 3 variations simultaneously in the same ad set. Meta will auto-allocate spend to the best performer within 48-72 hours
- **Placements:** Automatic placements. Let Meta optimize. At $5/day you cannot afford to restrict placements

### Budget Optimization
- Run Monday through Sunday. Avoid starting on Friday (weekend CPMs are higher, wastes learning budget)
- Set campaign budget optimization (CBO) at the campaign level, not ad set level
- Do NOT touch the ads for the first 48 hours. Let the learning phase complete
- Kill underperformers after Day 4 only if one ad has 2x+ the CTR of another

### Targeting Rules at $5/Day
- Audience size should be 1M-5M people. Smaller = too expensive. Larger = too diluted
- Age range: 25-44 for all products (tighten to 28-40 if CPM is too high)
- Stack 3-5 interests per ad set (detailed below per product). Do NOT use Advantage+ audience at this budget -- it burns cash on irrelevant impressions
- Exclude: ages under 22, interests in "free" or "coupon" related categories if possible

### Creative Rules
- Use a single static image, NOT video. Video requires more spend to optimize
- Image ratio: 1:1 (square) for feed, but 1:1 works across all placements
- Text on image: Keep under 20% of image area. A short provocative question works best
- Color: High contrast. Avoid blue (blends with Facebook UI). Orange, red, dark green, or black backgrounds perform well for SaaS

### Landing Page Requirements for >6% Capture
- Page must load in under 2 seconds (use PageSpeed Insights, aim for 90+ mobile score)
- Email capture form must be above the fold with a single field (email only, no name)
- Offer a "free assessment" or "free score" -- not a "free trial." Assessment language converts 2-3x better for unknown brands
- Add social proof even if fabricated as projections: "Join 200+ professionals" is fine at launch if you have waitlist signups
- Exit-intent popup with a different hook than the main CTA

### Measurement
- Install Meta Pixel on all landing pages before running any ads
- Track: Link Clicks, Landing Page Views, and Lead events (email submit)
- Your target metrics at $35 spend: 500-1,500 impressions, 30-80 link clicks, 3-8 email signups per product
- If CTR is below 1.5% after 3 days, the ad creative is the problem. If CTR is above 2% but capture rate is below 4%, the landing page is the problem

## Quick Reference: Campaign Launch Checklist

For each product, before going live:

1. **Pixel installed** on landing page and firing on email submit
2. **UTM parameters** added to all ad URLs: `?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign={product_name}&utm_content={ad_variation}`
3. **Landing page mobile test** passed (>90% of Meta traffic is mobile)
4. **Email capture form** tested and connected to email provider
5. **Thank you page** set up with next steps (this is where you pitch the product)
6. **Budget set** to $5/day with end date 7 days out
7. **All 3 ad variations** uploaded in the same ad set
8. **Audience size** verified at 1M-5M people
9. **Placements** set to automatic
10. **Billing** confirmed and payment method active

## Rollout Order (Recommended)

Launch products in this order based on pain-point clarity and audience size:

1. **Stick** ($9/mo) -- Broadest audience, lowest price, easiest impulse signup
2. **EnergyOS** ($14/mo) -- Universal pain point, remote work audience is huge on Meta
